## In-Depth Analysis of the Comparison

1. **Embodied AI Tier (The Precision Tier)**:
   - **Franka Research 3** and **Flexiv Rizon 4** represent the current academic standard for “physical contact” simulation. Their value lies not in handling tasks, but in the high-sensitivity force feedback at joints.
   - **Rokae xMate ER7 Pro** (note: not the CR series) became mature in 2026. With 7-axis torque sensing and a frequency of 1kHz, it has become the preferred alternative to Franka for domestic research groups.

2. **Lightweight and Mobility Tier (The Mobility Tier)**:
   - **Kinova Gen 3** is the most unique in this list. With a weight of 8.2kg but 7 degrees of freedom, it is the only true solution for AGV/mobile chassis.
   - **Hello Robot Stretch 3** has evolved to a NUC-12 computing version in 2026, with a load capacity increased to 2kg. It is specifically designed for grasping objects within “home narrow passages” and is a game-changer for service robot papers.

3. **Embodied AI Data Tier (The Scaling Tier)**:
   - **AgileX Piper** and **ViperX** exist to reproduce imitation learning algorithms such as ALOHA and GELLO. Their advantage is “low cost,” allowing laboratories to purchase 5–10 units simultaneously to build a data collection array (Robot Farm).

4. **Domestic integrated solutions**:
   - **D-Robotics MMK2** reflects the new trend in 2026: it no longer only sells robotic arms, but offers a complete system consisting of “computing power (RDK X5) + mobile chassis + arm,” aiming to lower the entry barrier for embodied AI.

## Comparison of Global Robot Manufacturers in 2026

The current market has formed a clear "tripartite confrontation" situation:

### 1. Established European and American Manufacturers

- **Manufacturers**: Franka Emika, Kinova, Universal Robots (UR).
- **Features**: The software ecosystem (driven by ROS/ROS 2) is highly mature, and reproduction of the code from papers is almost "zero effort".
- **Current Status**: Although the hardware is expensive and updates are slow, it still holds significant academic influence worldwide.

### 2. Chinese Manufacturers Balancing Performance and Cost

- **Manufacturers**: Rokae, Flexiv, Unitree - Z1 series.
- **Features**: Hardware parameters (load, force control) have caught up to or even surpassed those in Europe and America, and the price is highly competitive (usually 50% of foreign brands' prices).
- **Current Status**: By 2026, more and more UCAS research groups like yours will shift to **domestic high-end arms (such as xMate or Rizon)**, as localized technical support and deep adaptation to domestic AI chips (such as Ascend, Horizon) are lacking in foreign brands.

### 3. New Entrants Focused on Scalable Deployment

- **Vendors**: AgileX, UFACTORY, Ark Infinite.
- **Features**: Focus on extreme cost reduction, with emphasis on Python usability and open-source community integration (such as Datawhale and Every-Embodied).
- **Current Status**: Through the “low price + open source” strategy, they have quickly captured the imitation learning and desktop research market.
