## 5-Day System Learning Schedule

Instructor X will guide everyone through the process day by day, ensuring that daily goals are clear, learning content can be completed, and final outcomes can be implemented.

### Day 0: Opening Ceremony and Cognitive Warm-up

X will conduct an opening sharing session to introduce learning objectives, learning rhythm, data structure, and final output requirements. Everyone needs to prepare their GitHub accounts, platform access links, and study materials, and write down the most interesting embodied AI application scenarios that interest them.

### Day 1: Theory and Trends

Following Task 01《Theory and Trends》, understand the definition, development drivers, typical product forms, and industry challenges of embodied AI. The focus today is on creating the first cognitive map: what embodied AI is, and why it becomes important at this point in time.

### Day 2: Technical Analysis

Enter Task 02 "Technical Perspective" to systematically review core concepts such as VLA, VLM, world model, RynnBrain, and RynnRCP. X will further refine key issues like the "visual-language-action model", "world model", and "the relationship between platform capabilities and model capabilities".

### Day 3: Platform Demonstration and Practical Experience

Enter Task 03: "Demonstration Practice: From Idea to Implementation". Under the guidance of the instructor, log in to the DaMo Academy LeCloud Platform and observe the model marketplace, data management, model services, skill debugging, and deployment processes. The goal for today is not to achieve complex implementations, but to understand manually the basic workflow of an embodied AI task from concept to system.

### Day 4: Summary, Sharing, and Concept Output

Complete Task 04: "Summary and Sharing". Organize the learning insights from the past few days to create your own "Personal Application Blueprint". This blueprint should cover scenarios such as desktop organization, campus navigation, household shopping, laboratory supplies sorting, and auxiliary operations. The focus is on clearly outlining tasks, inputs, actions, routes, and challenges.
