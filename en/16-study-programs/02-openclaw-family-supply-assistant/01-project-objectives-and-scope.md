# Project Objectives and Scope

The goal of this topic is straightforward: to combine the frontends of OpenClaw, Feishu Notifications, Multi-dimensional Tables inventory, and Family Supplies Assistant into a tutorial chain that can be publicly read and reproduction.

## After completing this chapter

Readers should know:

1. Why is this topic not just about "robotic arm control," but a home supply workflow?
2. Which files need to be retained, and which ones must be excluded?
3. How will the subsequent chapters be structured for integration, synchronization, and display?

## Scope of this chapter

- Keep the tutorials, a small number of scripts, front-end source code, and schematic diagrams
- Exclude the scene editor, model weights, cache, download packages, temporary scripts, and keys
- Only include content that can be reproduction, and do not include internal debugging processes in the text
