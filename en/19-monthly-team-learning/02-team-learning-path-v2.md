- Course description:

Embodied AI is very trendy. From Mobile ALOHA to Pi0 and OpenVLA, SOTA models are updated almost every week. However, many people want to get involved but get stuck at the first step:

🤯 The materials are too fragmented; not sure where to start learning from?

🤯 The threshold is too high; the mathematical derivations and physics engine are exhausting?

🤯 Can't run, and the environment configuration is so complex that it makes you doubt everything?

Don’t panic! The beginner-friendly tutorial Every-Embodied: Zero to Hero, initiated by Datawhale and refined by the open-source community, has been officially opened sourced!🌟 Our focus is on: easy for beginners, reproducible in engineering, and adaptable to cutting-edge developments. It takes you from a single line of Python code, step by step, to equipping the robot with a “brain”!🧠

This learning path is adjusted to “begin with general knowledge, then branch out”: In the first half, study general knowledge topics such as an overview of embodied AI, robotics basics, control, visual perception, simulation, and reinforcement learning; in the second half, choose different directions based on interests. The branches no longer combine grasping, navigation, and cutting-edge reproduction, but are divided into three categories: **manipulation models and world model**, **navigation models and drone navigation**, and **applied practice and system deployment**. Those who enjoy robotic arm manipulation, grasping, VLA, ACT, Pi0, SmolVLA, OpenVLA, and world model can enter the manipulation models and world model branch; those who are interested in navigation, VLN, scene understanding, drone navigation, and spatial task planning can enter the navigation models and drone navigation branch; those who want to develop development boards, teleoperation, simulation tools, and multi-modal demos can enter the applied practice and system deployment branch.

​

- Target audience: Students, engineers, and researchers interested in embodied AI; those with a foundation in Python and basic mathematical knowledge. Beginners in Python / AI developers: Those who want to systematically transition into embodied AI fields; university students: Those needing course projects or final projects, or those aiming to quickly develop the ability to reproduce research results; hardware/robotics enthusiasts: Those seeking to establish a complete closed loop from physical machine control to Sim2Real deployment.
